Let’s build a better government, together.

Hi, I'm Mark. I'm a digital service leader, engineer, and community builder. For over ten years, I’ve worked across city, state, and federal government to help teams adopt modern ways of working, build smart tools, and deliver great public services that actually work for people. I've worked as an engineer at Google launching global products, a public interest technologist advising Congress on modernization, a research fellow closing the government's executive tech talent gap at Harvard, and a community builder bringing technology leaders together.

Software engineer by education, service designer by osmosis, coalition builder by nature.

How we can work together

Fixing Broken Services

If a critical process or software project isn't working right, I’ll drop in to figure out exactly what’s broken. I talk to the people using it, look at the technical backend, and give you a clear roadmap to turn things around.

Team Retreats & Strategy Workshops

I design and host immersive, interactive, small-group sessions to help your leadership team align on goals, fix internal friction, and build a forward-leaning workspace.

Designing Community Programs

If you want to build or improve a professional network, a fellowship, or a mentorship setup, I can help. I build the community structures, automate the behind-the-scenes software, and set up peer support.

Part-time Advising & Consulting

Sometimes you just need an experienced technologist on your side for a few hours a week. I provide independent strategic advice and technical reviews without the overhead of a full-time hire.
